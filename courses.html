Courses

Every QPR course teaches the same three steps. What changes is the depth, and who it is written for. Individual enrolment is handled by email while we move payments across — write to us and we will set you up within one working day.

Foundation

Start here. These are for everyone, take about an hour, and need no background in mental health.

QPR Gatekeeper Training

Who it is for
Anyone. No clinical background needed.
Length
About 1 hour
Format
Online, self-paced

The core course. Learn to recognise the warning signs of a suicide crisis, ask the question directly, and get someone to help. This is the course most people take, and the one institutions run across whole cohorts.

Ask about this course

QPR for Youth

Who it is for
School and college students, peer mentors
Length
About 1 hour
Format
Online, self-paced

The same three steps, written for a younger audience and for the situations that come up between friends and classmates rather than between colleagues.

Ask about this course

Advanced

For people who already have the basics, or who carry clinical responsibility.

QPR Pathfinder

Who it is for
Gatekeepers who want to go further
Length
Several hours
Format
Online, self-paced

Goes beyond the initial conversation into recognising longer-term risk, following up, and supporting someone through the period after a crisis rather than only during it.

Ask about this course

QPRT Suicide Risk Assessment and Management

Who it is for
Counsellors, psychologists, psychiatrists, clinical social workers
Length
Multi-hour, certified
Format
Online, self-paced

A structured clinical protocol for assessing suicide risk, documenting it defensibly and managing the case afterwards. Built for professionals who carry clinical responsibility for the outcome.

Ask about this course

Role-specific

The same method, written for the situations a particular role actually encounters.

QPR for Primary Care Providers

Who it is for
Doctors, nurses and clinic staff
Length
About 1 hour
Format
Online, self-paced

Most people who die by suicide have seen a doctor recently, often for something unrelated. This course covers detection and referral inside a short consultation.

Ask about this course

QPR for Educational Institutions

Who it is for
Wardens, faculty, counsellors, hostel staff
Length
About 1 hour
Format
Online, or in person for cohorts

For the people students actually go to first. Covers what to do inside an institution, including who to involve and how to hand over without breaking trust.

Ask about this course

QPR for Workplaces

Who it is for
HR, managers, workplace counsellors
Length
About 1 hour
Format
Online, or in person for cohorts

Recognising distress in a colleague, having the conversation without making it an HR process, and knowing where the handover goes.

Ask about this course

QPR for First Responders

Who it is for
Police, emergency services, ambulance crews
Length
About 1 hour
Format
Online, self-paced

Written for people who arrive at the scene, and who are themselves at elevated risk. Covers both directions.

Ask about this course

QPR Eldercare

Who it is for
Carers, geriatric staff, family members
Length
About 1 hour
Format
Online, self-paced

Older adults have among the highest suicide rates of any age group, and the warning signs look different. This course covers what changes with age.

Ask about this course

Instructor

QPR Gatekeeper Instructor Certification

Who it is for
Trainers, counsellors, programme leads
Length
Multi-day, certified
Format
Live, cohort-based

Certifies you to deliver QPR Gatekeeper Training yourself, within your own institution or community. This is how a programme scales: certify a small group of trainers, and they train everyone else.
